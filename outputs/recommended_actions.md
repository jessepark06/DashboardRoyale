# Recommended July actions ($100,000 budget)

## Decision

Do **not** replace the bottom 10 by house win. **9 of those 10** sit in Back Forty and average **99.7% availability**. They are quiet, not broken.

Fund **one** action: wayfinding for Back Forty. Cost **$7,500**. Base EV **$11,390** (NPV **$3,890**). Hold the other **$92,500**.

| Action | Qty | Cost | Base EV |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A-WAYFINDING — Wayfinding and signage refresh for a zone | 1 | $7,500 | $11,390 |

- Target: Back Forty (`Z-B40`). Only action aimed at the diagnosed cause. Assumes signs close 8% of the $6.20/hr theo gap vs the fleet over 20 days. Kill if realized lift is under 5%.

## Why not the other catalog items

- **Replace / buy the bottom 10.** Four purchased machines would be $88,000 and would still sit in a zone earning $11.96 theo/hr versus a fleet median of $18.15. M-1158 alone is **$-36,165 luck** with theo/hr in line with its zone.
- **Promo weekend ($15,000).** The weekday model already explains weekends. Buying a promo spends money to recreate demand the floor already has.
- **Technician or preventive maintenance.** Ridgeline faults (7.2 per machine) run above Halcyon (1.9), but July lead time leaves too few days for a $9,000–$11,520 package to pay back.
- **Moves.** Only four historical placement changes. A one-month move does not clear its $1,800 cost once cannibalization is allowed for.

## Sensitivity

- 8% of the zone gap (base): EV $11,390, NPV $3,890.
- 5% (kill line): EV $7,119, NPV $-381. Stop.
- 15%: EV $21,357.

## Measurement plan

- Pre-register Back Forty coin-in per available hour and theo per available hour.
- Compare July (after the 10-day lead) with matched weekdays in May–June, versus Main Entrance and Center Pit as controls.
- Kill rule: if the zone closes less than 5% of its theo/hr gap, do not scale signs or moves.
